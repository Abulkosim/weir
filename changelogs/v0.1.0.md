# v0.1.0

2026-10-24 · [Download](https://github.com/Abulkosim/weir/releases/tag/v0.1.0)

- Test download, upload and latency from the menu bar or system tray against Cloudflare, with a live line while it runs. A test takes about 20 seconds and never uses more than 600 MB.
- See latency under load next to idle latency, and a plain verdict such as "Good for 4K video and calls". Open it for video, calls, games and how the line holds up when busy.
- On a hotspot, Low Data Mode or a metered connection, Weir asks before testing and says how much data it will use.
- The tray icon fills while a test runs and shows the last result's health at rest; it is crossed out when you are offline. Settings → Tray & startup can add the speed next to the icon on macOS.
- Every test is kept on this computer for a year. History shows them by day; export them as CSV from History or Settings.
- Updates are checked once a day and installed only when you choose, in Settings → Updates.
