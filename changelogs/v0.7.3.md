# v0.7.3

2026-11-10 · [Download](https://github.com/Abulkosim/weir/releases/tag/v0.7.3)

- On a hotspot or metered connection, the data estimate shows the most a test can use, "Uses up to 600 MB", because your recent results may come from a different network.
- Weir no longer replaces settings or History it couldn't open, or History saved by a newer version. The popover says so instead.
- On Windows, the popover can no longer freeze while a test is running.
- History's summary covers the same recent tests it names, and slow hours that run past midnight show as one period.
- Exported CSV, the PDF report and `weir --json` give each time its own UTC offset across daylight saving changes.
- The connection check gives up on a router that doesn't answer after about 5 seconds instead of 15.
- A failed update check is retried within 15 minutes instead of a day later. If your shortcut can't be set when Weir starts, the popover tells you.
- With the keyboard or a screen reader, focus stays in the popover when pages change, and messages are announced.
