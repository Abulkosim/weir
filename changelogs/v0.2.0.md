# v0.2.0

2026-10-24 · [Download](https://github.com/Abulkosim/weir/releases/tag/v0.2.0)

- Check connection answers "is it me or the internet?" in one sentence, for example "Your Wi-Fi is fine. The problem is past your router." It reads your Wi-Fi signal, router, DNS server and Cloudflare in a few seconds and uses about 10 kB. Open it with the pulse button at the top of the popover, from a failed test, or from the offline notice.
- Settings → Outages → Watch for outages, off by default, checks about once a minute with a tiny request. Outages appear in History as "No internet until 14:09 · 7 min" and in the CSV export, and you can be notified when the connection drops and returns.
- History shows download by hour of day and names the slowest stretch, for example "Slowest around 20:00–22:00".
- The live number keeps counting during a test even when the window is in the background.
