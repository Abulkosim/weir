# v0.8.0

2026-11-20 · [Download](https://github.com/Abulkosim/weir/releases/tag/v0.8.0)

- With Settings → Outages → Watch for outages on, History says where each outage broke: no network connection, at your router, or past it. The notification, the provider report and the CSV say the same.
- On Windows, an outage at your provider is no longer recorded as your computer having no network.
- "Connection back" is no longer sent while your computer is still offline.
- Upload results are more accurate. Slow connections no longer stall, and far-away servers read much closer to the real speed.
- A test on a fast connection can use up to 1 GB, so gigabit links finish measuring instead of stopping early. On a hotspot the estimate reads "Uses up to 1 GB".
