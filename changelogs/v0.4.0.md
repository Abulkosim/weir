# v0.4.0

2026-10-26 · [Download](https://github.com/Abulkosim/weir/releases/tag/v0.4.0)

- Enter the speeds you pay for in Settings → Your plan, and each result says how close you get, for example "62% of your 500 Mbps plan". History and the tray tooltip show it too.
- Report in History saves a PDF of the last 30 days for your provider: medians against your plan, how often it fell under half, the slowest hours, outages, a chart and every test. Also in Settings → History → Save PDF.
- Scheduled tests, off by default: Settings → Scheduled tests runs a full test every 6 or 12 hours or once a day. It shows the data it will use each month and never runs on a hotspot, a metered connection, Low Data Mode or Low Power Mode. Scheduled results have a clock icon in History.
- The popover no longer shifts by a pixel after resizing to fit its content.
